The Problem

According to the World Health Organization, 2.2 billion people worldwide do not have safe drinking water. In the Philippines, 1 in 10 families still struggles to access clean water, and 1 in 5 lacks basic sanitation facilities.

Our Advocacy

Our mission supports SDG 6: ensuring access to clean water and sanitation for all. We believe water is a right, not a privilege.

About Me

I’m Scotty Louie D. Depositario, a scholar who believes in using knowledge and education to make a difference. Through this project, I want to raise awareness about clean water and sanitation, and show that even students can contribute to positive change in our communities.

What We Do

We try to raise awareness about this ongoing problem, not only in the Philippines, but also in the world.
