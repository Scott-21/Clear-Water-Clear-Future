Issues in the World:

  1. Water Scarcity: With water being a finite resource growing in demand, as the population around the world increases, we may struggle in keeping up with the water supply demand.
  2. Water Pollution: Poor water quality being used, leading to contamination from different microbes, leading to diseases.
  3. Ecosystem Degration: Issues like pollution, climate change, and even water scarcity could cause habitat destruction, species endangerment and even a loss of biodiversity.

Issues in the Philippines:

  1. Lack of Access to Safe Water and Sanitation: According to UN and UNICEF data, featured by the planet water organization, around 53% of households lack access to safely managed water, and 39% lack safe sanitation. Schools have even worse access, with 55% lacking safe water.
  2. Waterborne diseases: Pollution mixed with the constant environmental challenges like typhoons leads to increased risks of illnesses like diarrhea, typhoid, fever and more.
  3. Severe Plastic Pollution: The Philippines is one of the main consumers of single-use plastic, using things like plastic sachets can clog up waterways, and even some of the plastic waste ending up on rivers and oceans, threatening the marine ecosystem and the health of the public.
How to Help:

Solutions:

  1. Reduce water waste by turning off the tap while brushing teeth or washing dishes.
  2. Collect rainwater for watering plants or cleaning outdoor areas.
  3. Participate in or organize local clean-ups to help keep rivers and waterways clear.
  4. Report broken pipes, leaks, or illegal dumping to local authorities or water districts.
  5. Educate family and friends about water conservation and proper waste disposal.

Things being done currently:

  1. UNICEF is working with local governments to install safe water stations in schools.
  2. NGOs like Water.org provide microloans to help families access clean water systems.
  3. The Philippines is expanding piped water coverage through local water districts.
  4. Community clean-up drives help reduce plastic waste in waterways.
  5. Public campaigns promote water conservation and sustainable usage.
